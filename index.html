OEE Dashboard
Proof-of-concept demonstration — figures are simulated, not live plant data
OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) answers: “Of the time we planned to make parts, how much actually became good product?”   Formula: OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality
—
Availability
Share of planned shift time the machines were actually running, not idle or down
—
Performance
How close cycle times are to the expected rate when a machine is running
—
Quality
Good parts as a share of all parts made (scrap and rework count against this)
—
Shop OEE
Availability × Performance × Quality for the whole shop today
Shop floor map
Each badge is that machine’s live OEE. Hover (or tab to) a badge for Availability, Performance, Quality and status.
